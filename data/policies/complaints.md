# Making a complaint
Document: POL-COMP | Version 1.0 | Synthetic policy for a portfolio simulation. Not the policy of any real bank.

## How to make a complaint
You can make a complaint by phone, in writing, or by asking our assistant to pass you to a member of our team. You do not need to use any particular words, and making a complaint will never affect how we treat you.

## What happens after you make a complaint
We will acknowledge your complaint promptly and try to resolve it as quickly as possible. If we cannot resolve your complaint within three working days, we will send you a written final response within eight weeks.

## Taking your complaint to the Financial Ombudsman Service
If you are unhappy with our final response to your complaint, or we have not responded within eight weeks, you can refer it to the Financial Ombudsman Service. This is a free and independent service. You must normally do this within six months of our final response.

## How our assistant handles complaints
Our assistant cannot resolve complaints itself. If you tell it you want to complain, or that you are unhappy with our service, it will pass your conversation to a member of our team with a summary, so you do not have to repeat yourself.
