# Fees and repaying your loan early
Document: POL-FEES | Version 1.0 | Synthetic policy for a portfolio simulation. Not the policy of any real bank.

## Arrangement fees on our loans
None of our loans have an arrangement fee. The only other charges are those described here, if you miss a payment or repay your loan early.

## Repaying your loan early
You can repay all or part of your loan early at any time. A charge of up to 58 days' interest may apply. We will tell you the exact amount before you decide to go ahead.

## Missed or late loan payments
If you miss a loan payment, we may charge a fee of £12, and the missed payment may be reported to credit reference agencies, which could affect your ability to borrow in future. If you are worried about making a payment, please contact us as early as possible, because we have options that can help.

## How interest is charged on our loans
Our loans have a fixed interest rate, so your monthly repayment stays the same for the whole term. The rate you are offered depends on your credit profile and may be different from the representative APR shown in our product information.
