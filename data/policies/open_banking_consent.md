# Open Banking and how we use your data
Document: POL-DATA | Version 1.0 | Synthetic policy for a portfolio simulation. Not the policy of any real bank.

## What Open Banking is
Open Banking is a secure, regulated way for you to share your bank transaction data with another provider. You give permission through your own bank, and we never see or store your online banking login details.

## What we use your Open Banking data for
With your permission, we read your last three months of bank transactions to verify your income and regular outgoings for your loan application. We do not use this data for marketing, and we do not share it with anyone else.

## Giving and withdrawing consent for Open Banking
We will always ask for your consent before reading your bank transactions. Your consent applies only to your current loan application. You can withdraw it at any time, but we will then be unable to make an automatic decision, and we will offer to pass your application to a member of our team.
