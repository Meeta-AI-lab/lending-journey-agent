# Customers who need extra support
Document: POL-VULN | Version 1.0 | Synthetic policy for a portfolio simulation. Not the policy of any real bank.

## Our commitment to customers who need extra support
Anyone can find themselves needing extra support, for example because of their health, a major life event such as a bereavement or losing a job, money worries, or finding financial products hard to understand. We want every customer to get a fair outcome, and we adapt how we help you.

## Signs that a customer may need extra support
Our assistant listens for signs that you may need extra support, such as mentioning serious money difficulties, illness, a bereavement, or feeling pressured by someone else to borrow. It does not try to diagnose or judge your situation.

## What happens if you may need extra support
If our assistant notices signs that you may need extra support, it will offer to pass you to a trained member of our team instead of continuing an automated application. You can also ask to speak to a person at any time, for any reason.

## Free and independent help with money worries
If you are struggling with money, free and impartial help is available from MoneyHelper and from debt charities such as StepChange and National Debtline. Borrowing more is not always the right answer to financial difficulty.
