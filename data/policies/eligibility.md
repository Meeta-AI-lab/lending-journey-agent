# Who can apply for a loan
Document: POL-ELIG | Version 1.0 | Synthetic policy for a portfolio simulation. Not the policy of any real bank.

## Basic eligibility criteria for a personal loan
To apply for any of our loans you must be aged 18 or over, live in the UK, and have a UK bank account that receives your income. You also need a regular take-home income of at least £1,000 a month, which we verify from your bank transactions.

## Credit history and eligibility for a loan
We look at your credit history as part of every loan application. We cannot offer a loan if you have had a default recorded in the last 36 months, or if your credit profile falls into our lowest risk band. A declined application does not stop you applying again in future, and we will always explain the main reason.

## What you can use a loan for
Our loans can be used for personal purposes such as buying a car, home improvements, a holiday, a wedding, or paying off existing debts. We do not lend for business purposes, investments, gambling, or a deposit on a property.

## Why a loan application might be declined
The most common reasons are an income below our minimum, a recent default, a loan amount that is not affordable based on your income and outgoings, or a purpose we do not lend for. If we decline your application, we will tell you the main reason and suggest options, such as a smaller amount or speaking to a member of our team.

## Applying for a loan if you are self-employed or your income varies
You can apply if you are self-employed or your income changes from month to month. We normally use the average of your last three months of income. If your income varies by more than 30% between months, we use your lowest month instead, so that repayments stay affordable in a quieter month.

## If we cannot verify your income
We need at least three months of bank transaction history to verify your income. If we have less than this, we cannot make an automatic decision, and we will pass your application to a member of our team to review.
